Detailed Description

the representation of a field within a message.

Warning
A MessageField borrows its FieldIdentity; it does not own one. For a decoded message the identity belongs to a FieldInstruction inside the Codecs::TemplateRegistry, so the registry must outlive every Message decoded from it, and every Field, FieldSet and Sequence reached through one. Callers that build messages by hand are equally responsible for keeping their identities alive.

Storing the identity by value would make this safe, but it costs about 15% of decode time; the identity was never designed for shared ownership even though it is immutable at encode and decode time.
